# VISION: Next-Gen AI Tutor Agent (GyanVerse Academy)

## 1. Project Goal
To build a highly intelligent, interactive, and strict-yet-friendly AI Tutor for school students in Classes 1 through 10 (supporting GSEB and CBSE boards on Windows and Android platforms). The agent eliminates the need for traditional extra tuition by acting as a personalized 1-on-1 teacher.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Primary Users:** Students in Classes 1 through 10 (GSEB and CBSE boards).
- **Secondary Users (Monitors):** Parents and guardians who need assurance that their kids are studying effectively and receiving structured academic support.

## 3. Core Persona of the AI Tutor
- **Strict but Friendly:** Approachable for doubts but maintains learning discipline.
- **Logical Verifier:** Does NOT blindly agree with the student. It analyzes the student's input and only agrees if the concept or answer is genuinely correct.
- **Guiding, Not Spoon-feeding:** Explains concepts in simple language but forces the student to think and solve the final piece of the puzzle.

## 4. Key Features & Scope Alignment
1. **Canonical Product Scope:**
   - **Public Product Name:** GyanVerse Academy
   - **Internal Engine:** AI Tutor Buddy
   - **Supported Classes:** Classes 1 through 10
   - **Supported Production Boards:** GSEB and CBSE
   - **Target Platforms:** Windows and Android
2. **Implementation vs Acceptance Boundaries:**
   - Implemented core source logic and automated test suites.
   - Pending real curriculum package acquisition and complete coverage.
   - Pending Windows EXE build acceptance, Android APK build acceptance, and physical-device testing.
3. **Daily Revision & Homework Engine:**
   - Takes daily input: "Today I learned [Topic] in [Subject]."
   - Generates customized revision questions and assigns homework based on the topic.
   - Evaluates completed homework and provides constructive feedback.
4. **Voice Command System:** Two-way voice interaction for a natural, conversational teaching experience with offline fallback.
5. **Anti-Distraction & Parent Alerts:**
   - Tracks study progress and focus.
   - Guardian-safe progress reporting and privacy-conscious alerts.
