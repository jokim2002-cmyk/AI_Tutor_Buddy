# Phase 6H — Learning Intelligence & Exam Readiness

Adds evidence-based learning intelligence above Phase 6G memory and knowledge graph.

## Capabilities
- concept and subject mastery aggregation
- syllabus coverage
- learning velocity and consistency
- prerequisite impact
- exam-readiness score with evidence confidence and uncertainty
- prioritized revision plans
- teacher, Class Teacher, Principal, and guardian-safe summaries
- non-deterministic prediction policy

Readiness is a support estimate, never a permanent label or guaranteed exam prediction.
